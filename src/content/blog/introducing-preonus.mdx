## Why the Name "Preonus"?

In the Standard Model of particle physics, **quarks** and **leptons** are treated as elementary particles. Yet physicists noticed striking patterns in their charges, masses, and generations—leading to a deeper hypothesis: what if quarks themselves are not fundamental, but composed of even smaller, point-like sub-particles?

In 1974, physicists Jogesh Pati and Abdus Salam coined the term **preon** for these hypothetical sub-quark constituents—the layer *underneath* what was previously thought to be fundamental.

<Blockquote>
	<Fragment slot="quote">
		“A great name should tell you what something is made of. Preons are the smallest conceivable primitives—stripped of all excess weight, indivisible, and foundational.”
	</Fragment>
	<Fragment slot="caption">— The Preonus Brand Philosophy</Fragment>
</Blockquote>

The name **Preonus** brings together two deliberate ideas:
1. **Preon (*The Fundamental Primitive*)**: Named after sub-quark particles—representing the smallest, purest, indivisible units from which larger structures are built.
2. **-us (*Unity & Craft*)**: A classical, cohesive suffix that turns a scientific term into a timeless engineering proper noun—standing alongside names like *Quarkus* and *Micronaut* while going one quantum tier deeper.

Every polygon, stencil cut, and circle in the mark has a specific meaning:

### 1. The Outer Silhouette: The Reduced Planck Constant (ħ)
Look at the overarching silhouette of the icon—it is a bold, architectural rendering of **ħ (*h-bar*)**, the **Reduced Planck Constant**.
- **What it is:** In quantum mechanics, `ħ = h / 2π` is the fundamental constant that defines the quantum scale of action and angular momentum—the exact scale at which subatomic particles exist.
- **Why it is here:** The vertical stem and curved arch form the lowercase **$h$**, anchoring the mark in both physics notation and strong typographic geometry.

### 2. The 24° Diagonal Crossbar (The Planck Slash)
Cutting cleanly across the upper vertical stem at a **24° angle** (`27,78.5 → 105,44.5`) is the signature bar that transforms a plain letter **$h$** into **ħ**.
- **What it indicates:** This slash is the defining visual signature of quantum physics. Its upward 24° angle introduces forward kinetic momentum into an otherwise grounded vertical pillar.

### 3. The 6-Unit Parallel Stencil Cuts
Look closely where the diagonal bar crosses the vertical stem: the bar does not simply overlap the stem—it **slices cleanly through it**, leaving two razor-sharp, parallel **6-unit negative-space channels** above and below the slash.
- **What it indicates:** Precision engineering and structural clarity. These parallel stencil breaks ensure that even when the icon is shrunk down to a `16 × 16 px` browser favicon, the ħ glyph never blurs or bleeds into a solid blob.

### 4. The Inner Cluster: The 2 + 1 Preon Triplet (TTV)
Nested inside the hollow of the arch sit three circular dots arranged in an asymmetric triangle—representing the **Harari–Shupe Rishon Model**, one of the most famous preon theories in physics, where an up quark is formed by a bound triplet of **two T-rishons and one V-rishon (TTV)**:
- **Top-Left Dot (`cx: 116, cy: 148`, `r: 10.5`) — First T-Rishon**: A charged constituent particle carrying `+1/3 e` electric charge.
- **Bottom-Left Dot (`cx: 116, cy: 184`, `r: 10.5`) — Second T-Rishon**: Paired vertically with the first T-rishon to establish the symmetric `2` in the `2 + 1` triplet.
- **Right Offset Dot (`cx: 147, cy: 166`, `r: 9`) — The V-Rishon**: Slightly smaller and offset to the right, representing the electrically neutral (`0` charge) constituent particle that completes the bound state.

In particle physics, preons are modeled as **zero-dimensional, point-like particles**—which is why circular dots in a bound triangular cluster are the exact geometric representation of a composite quark interior.

### 5. The Enclosing Arch & Continuous Violet Field
The thick, balanced `34`-unit arch curves around the `2 + 1` preon cluster, visually representing **confinement**—the physical boundary holding constituent particles together as a single, unified system.

Across the entire mark—and continuing seamlessly across the **Preonus** wordmark in our horizontal lockup—flows a single, uninterrupted **Quantum Violet Field**:
- **Cosmic Light Violet (`#DDD6FE`)**: Illuminates the upper ħ cap and diagonal slash, ensuring crisp contrast against dark backgrounds.
- **Electric Lilac (`#A78BFA`)**: Bridges the central arch and the `"us"` suffix of the wordmark, uniting symbol and typography.
- **Deep Quantum Indigo (`#6D28D9`)**: Anchors the base of the stems and the preon core, giving the entire identity depth across both dark and light themes.
